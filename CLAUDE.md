# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## Overview

"NOS Dourados" (NOS = Núcleo de Organização Social, provisional name) is a static, no-build site (plain HTML/CSS/vanilla ES modules, no package.json, no tests, no linter) of five pages:

- **Início** (`index.html`, `js/home.js`; redesigned and built 2026-11-12 from `design/inicio-proposta.html`): the city's question on the sky of this hour, "O que está acontecendo em Dourados?", with a line of what the site holds under it; on the land the very next activity, labelled "A próxima atividade" (its day circled by hand with "hoje"/"amanhã" and the time beside it, its title opening it in the agenda, `agenda.html#<id>`, and under 760px the whole block does; who, when and where; "Mandar no WhatsApp", and from 760px "Ver na agenda"), "Ver todas as N atividades", then what the other pages hold, as rows of type (Notícias: the story the page leads with; the Mapa: how many places, and its layers' drawings; Comércio: how many businesses, and their categories), and who NOS follows ("O NOS acompanha o que 85 organizações de Dourados publicam", static in `index.html`, with the eight groups' dots).
- **Agenda** (`agenda.html`, `js/app.js`; `calendario.html` until 2026-10-27, now a page that forwards to it): one permanent calendar of the activities of the social movements, unions, student groups, Indigenous and quilombola organizations, councils and parties of Dourados/MS, as a list of days or a month. For each activity it generates a WhatsApp-ready message and a 1080×1350 PNG flyer, and saves it in the person's own calendar ("Salvar na minha agenda"); "Salvar tudo na agenda" subscribes them to the whole agenda (`agenda.ics`). Called "Agenda" in the UI since the Céu · Terra redesign (2026-10-27); the address stays `calendario.html`. Until 2026-10-26 it was `index.html`.
- **Notícias** (`noticias.html`, `js/noticias.js`; built 2026-11-05 from the approved artboards): what the organizations are living (Conquista, Perda, Alerta, Informe; public-service alerts too), picked by the NOS team from their posts, each story with who lived it, its source post and, when it has one, its activity in the agenda; the story the team leads with ("Destaque"), then the weeks, filters by organization and kind, a story opening in the agenda's panel with its WhatsApp message.
- **Mapa** (`mapa.html`, `js/mapa.js`; built 2026-11-11 from idea C · Camadas of the round on the canvas): the city's map of culture and of the movements, by layers (`MAP_LAYERS`: Capoeira, which is the Mapa da Capoeira of COMAFRO Dourados, then Batalhas de rima, Feiras, Espaços culturais, Sindicatos, Universidades, Poder público), each turned on and off (chips on phones, a rail on the desk); the places are points with their layer's drawing on a MapLibre map painted in Céu · Terra, merged into counts where they crowd; a place opens in a card (its times, contacts, who reviewed it and when, "Enviar no WhatsApp", "Como chegar"); the panel lists every place by layer and by name, and is the page when the map can't open.
- **Comércio** (`comercio.html`, `js/comercio.js`): the businesses that answered the network's registration form (a Google Form), A to Z, each with what it offers, its WhatsApp numbers (with a phone glyph to call), Instagram and links, search, category chips and an "Indicar no WhatsApp" message. It was paused ("Em breve") from 2026-10-27 to 2026-10-28; the home's door shows how many businesses there are.

Language rule: development (code, identifiers, CSS classes, comments, commit messages) in English; all content and UI strings in Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR). Content-like slugs (flyer PNG filenames: `folha`, `mes`, `cartaz`, `cancelada`; the query values `?tema=escuro`, `?ceu=noite`) stay in pt-BR.

Product truth lives in `PRODUCT.md`; the visual direction ("Céu · Terra": the sky of Dourados at this hour over a woven band, the land below) and the direction contracts live in `.impeccable/surfaces/` (`index-html.md` for the home, `agenda-html.md` for the agenda, `noticias-html.md` for Notícias, `mapa-html.md` for the Mapa, `comercio-html.md` for Comércio); `DESIGN.md` (+ `.impeccable/design.json`) documents the built design system. The design source is `design/`: one HTML file per artboard (the approved Céu · Terra artboards, the other directions of the round, the band's research, the mark, the four flyers), all shown on one zoomable canvas by htmlboard (see "Designs"). Use the project's impeccable skill for UI work.

## Running

The pages must be served over HTTP: ES modules and the `fetch` of the CSVs fail under `file://` (an inline script in `agenda.html`, `noticias.html` and `comercio.html` shows an error notice in that case).

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000   # then open http://localhost:8000
```

Deploy = push `main`: GitHub Pages publishes the folder from `main` / root (its Jekyll build leaves hidden folders such as `.claude/`, `.impeccable/` and `.githooks/` out); any other static host works the same way. Some files are generated, committed and rebuilt by the repository's pre-commit hook in every commit that changes their data (see "The generated files" under "Git workflow"): each folder of month files' `index.json` (`data/agenda/`, `data/noticias/`), the index of the months the pages read (see "Month files"), and `agenda.ics` at the root, the calendar "Salvar tudo na agenda" subscribes people to (calendar apps fetch it from the site on their own, so it has to be a real published file). It is live at https://nos.dourados.social (`CNAME`); `SITE_URL` in `js/config.js` and the link-preview `canonical`/`og:url`/`og:image` tags of the four pages hold that address.

## Pages and navigation

- Five HTML files linked with plain `<a href="agenda.html">` links, so any static host serves them (no router, no rewrites; GitHub Pages also answers `/agenda` without the extension, but links and messages always use the file name). `index.html` is the root. `calendario.html` is the agenda's old address (until 2026-10-27): a stub that forwards to `agenda.html` keeping the query and the hash, so links already shared in WhatsApp groups and old shortcuts keep working; keep it, and its line in `sw.js`'s SHELL.
- Every page opens with the same header on the sky (`header.site-head`): the sun or the moon and the stars (`.sky-art`, `aria-hidden`), then `.site-head-inner` with the bar (`div.site-bar`: the brand, `a.brand`, the NOS mark and "Dourados", "(página inicial)" for screen readers; the pages' links, `nav.site-nav`, one `a.nav-link` per page, the open one with `aria-current="page"`; the theme button, `button.theme-toggle`, `data-theme-toggle`), and the page's name (`.page-title`: `h1.page-name` and the line `#page-sub`, "4 atividades até 20/11" on the agenda) or, on the home, its question (`.ask`: `h1.ask-title` and the line `p.ask-sub`). Phones (under 760px): the page's name beside the theme button in a grid (`.site-bar` steps aside with `display: contents`), no brand (the home shows it), and `nav.site-nav` becomes a bar fixed along the foot of the screen (the body pads for it); from 760px, and on the home, `.site-head-inner` is a column: the bar (the brand, the links and the button in 68px), then the name or the question with the sky under it. One `nav` serves both places. Under the header, the band (`div.band`, the horizon); at the foot of every page `footer.site-foot` with the full name and the band's provisional credit. The home's brand carries `aria-current="page"` too.
- The header sticks to the top of the screen, down to the band, while the page scrolls ("The header sticks" in `style.css`, `js/head.js`; since 2026-11-06, waiting for the user's approval). The inner pages on phones keep it whole (112px with the band). From 760px and on the home it folds: the header is `position: sticky` at `top: -var(--fold)`, the bar is sticky inside it (`top: env(safe-area-inset-top)`), so the rest slides up under the bar and stops with `--head-keep` (22px from 760px) of sky under it, the last bands and the sun or the moon showing: 108px on the inner pages, 134px on the home (on phones the home keeps its bar and band, 90px). What sits under the bar (the page's name and line; the home's question and its line and, at night, the home's moon) fades out over the first 16px of scroll with a scroll-driven animation (`animation-timeline: scroll(root)`, `!important` so the reduced-motion rule doesn't stop it: it follows the scroll, it has no time of its own); where scroll timelines aren't supported, `head.js` sets `data-scrolled` on `<html>` and it hides at once. The header draws the band too (`.site-head::after`, under its box, lying exactly on `div.band` at rest, both sized by `--band-h`). Nothing changes the page's height, so nothing jumps. `initHead()` (called by `initPage`) measures `--fold` (the header's height under the bar, less `--head-keep`) on the header and `--head-stuck` (what stays on screen, with the band) on `<html>`, again on resize and when the header's size changes; the desk's panel (`.bench`) and Comércio's card (`.side`) stick under `--head-stuck`, their heights and the flyer preview's size subtract it, and `html`'s `scroll-padding-top` keeps focus, `scrollIntoView` and `#` links clear of it. Short screens (under 501px tall: phones on their side) don't stick at all (`--head-stuck` 0). The design artboards that copy the site's header load `js/head.js` too.
- `index.html`'s head holds the notes on the shared tags (browser colour, theme and sky, icons, manifest, home-screen app, link preview); `agenda.html`, `noticias.html`, `mapa.html` and `comercio.html` repeat the tags with a pointer there, changing only the title, descriptions and addresses. The five heads carry the same inline script (theme and sky before the first paint) and the same two `theme-color` metas.
- `js/page.js` is what the pages share: `$`, `el`, `escapeHtml`, `safeUrl`, `initPage(name)` (the brand from `js/config.js`, the footer's full name, the tab title "Comércio · NOS Dourados", the icons of `[data-icon]` nodes, `initSky()` and `initHead()`), `showNotice`/`showOffline(savedAt, what)`/`announce` (`#notice`, `#live`), `copyText(text)` (the clipboard, with a fallback that works inside an open dialog), `readSheet(url)` (fetch with the cache fallback, `decodeCSV`, `parseCSV`, plus `savedAt` from the worker's `X-Cached-At`), `readJSON(url)` (the same for a JSON file), `monthlySource(dir, build)` (a folder of month files, `js/month-files.js`, with those readers and the folder's builder) with `agendaSource()` and `newsSource()`, and `registerWorker()`. `js/sky.js`: `skyPeriod(date)`, `greeting(date)` and `initSky()` (see "Light, dark and the sky"). `js/head.js`: `initHead()`, the header sticking (above).

## Data

The agenda is a folder of Brazilian CSVs (`;`, UTF-8 BOM, CRLF, dd/mm/aaaa), `AGENDA_DIR` in `js/config.js` (`data/agenda/`): one file per month, all with the same columns (see "Month files" below). Columns (pt-BR headers, matched loosely by `COLUMN_ALIASES` in `js/events.js`: accents/case/spaces normalized, English aliases accepted): `Título`, `Dia` (required; a full date with the year — yearless dates are rejected so they can't change meaning at New Year); `Dia final` (multi-day; a yearless value takes the start's year, or the next one if it would fall before the start), `Horário`, `Término` (both accept `9h`, `9h30`, `09:30` and spreadsheet time cells like `14:00:00`/`2:00 PM`; an end time alone renders "até 12h"; no time at all renders "Horário a confirmar", never "dia todo", except for a period of several days (a `Dia final`) and for the types in `UNTIMED_TYPES` (`js/config.js`: Prazo and Campanha, i.e. deadlines, enrolment and consultation periods and collection campaigns, which never get a time), which show no time at all: `awaitsTime` in `js/events.js`, used by the cards, the panel, the home, the save menu, the calendar notes and the flyers), `Tipo`, `Organização` (split on `,`/`;` outside parentheses, main first → `event.orgs`), `Setor` (alias `Categoria`), `Local`, `Cidade` (empty, `HOME_CITY` or `HOME_CITY` + state = here → `event.city` is `''`), `Descrição`, `Contato`, `Link` (action link), `Fonte` (source post), `Visível` (unchecked = draft, skipped silently, `isHidden`), `Cancelado` + `Motivo Cancelamento` (`isChecked`), and `Notas internas` (ignored by the site). `parseCSV` (`js/csv.js`; the Windows-1252 fallback is in `decodeCSV`) returns each record's spreadsheet row (`lines`) so notices name the right file and "linha N" ("2026-11.csv, linha 5") even after blank rows. `buildEvents` (run on each month's file) returns `{ events, invalid, warnings, missing }`: rows without a title or a full date are reported (folded list in the notice), an unreadable `Dia final` keeps the row as one-day with a warning, and missing `Título`/`Dia` headers produce one error naming them; the pre-commit hook refuses all of these, so the notice only shows for files that skipped it. The rows were extracted (and adversarially verified) from an Instagram survey of the organizations (`../coleta-instagram/*.md`, next to the project folder and kept outside this repository, whose `../scripts/merge_agenda.py` folds a checkup's new rows into the month files) and the raw captions; their `Notas internas` say what is uncertain and why rows are hidden. `agendaSummary(events, now)` (the agenda's top line and door: "4 atividades até 20/11", cancelled ones left out) and `orgFullName(org)` (`ORG_NAMES`) live in `js/events.js`.

The news is the same kind of folder, `NEWS_DIR` (`data/noticias/`), one file per month by `Data`. Columns (matched loosely by `newsColumnMap` in `js/news.js`): `Título` (the headline, written by the team: NOS speaking, so the page sets it in Bricolage) and `Data` (the day of the fact, or of the post when the fact has none; full date) required; `Tipo` (Conquista, Perda, Alerta, Informe: `NEWS_KINDS` in `js/config.js` orders the chips and gives their plural; another kind still shows), `Organização` (as the agenda's), `Setor`, `Resumo` (a sentence or two: the card, the panel's lead, the message), `Texto` (the story: paragraphs apart by a blank line inside the cell; claims attributed, "segundo o SIMTED…"), `Fonte` (the source post), `Atividade` (an agenda activity's id, `ev-…`: the card and the panel link to it, "Na agenda"; its month's agenda file is read for that), `Destaque` (checked: the page leads with the newest such story, and so does the home's door), `Visível` and `Notas internas` as in the agenda. `buildNews` returns `{ events, invalid, warnings, missing }` like `buildEvents`; ids are `nt-<dateKey>-<slug>`. The first 15 stories (2026-08 to 2026-10) were taken from the survey reports with every quote checked against them; their `Notas internas` name the report and what is uncertain, and the candidates not used are in `../checkup/2026-11-05/noticias/candidatas.md`.

### Month files

So that a visit never downloads a whole list that only grows, the agenda and the news are folders read a month at a time (`js/month-files.js`, no DOM, shared with the tools):

- `AAAA-MM.csv` holds the rows whose day falls in that month: the agenda's activities by the day they start (`Dia`; a period that runs into the next month stays in the month it starts), the news by `Data`; `sem-data.csv` holds the drafts that still have no date, hidden (`Visível` FALSE; no page reads it). Every file of a folder has the same header row. A new row goes in its month's file (a new month's file starts with a copy of another month's header); a row whose day changes month moves to the new month's file.
- `index.json` is generated from them (`tools/month-files.mjs`, run by the pre-commit hook; never edit it): `{ "months": [{ "month": "2026-10", "until": "2026-11-08" }, …] }`, one entry per month with something to show (a month of hidden rows only is left out), `until` being the last day its rows reach.
- A page reads the index, then only the months its window needs: from a first day on, every month whose `until` reaches it, so an agenda period that started months ago is read while it runs; the reader's `events` are the rows that reach the first day or later, merged in date order (`mergeEvents`; ids are made per file, `ev-<dateKey>-<slug>` for the agenda, the same as when it was one file). The agenda opens from the 1st of last month (`openingDay` in `app.js`: this month's page is whole, grey days included, and "Ver N atividades anteriores" has a month behind it), the news from the 1st of the month before last (three months; `openingDay` in `noticias.js`), the home from today (its door: the last two months of news). `extend(day)` moves the first day back (at once when the months it needs are read already, else a promise); `older()` names the month before the window; `refresh()` reads the window again, the index too. `open`/`refresh` are all or nothing: a month that can't be read fails the load.
- Checks (`node tools/month-files.mjs`, also in the hook, for every folder of `tools/folders.mjs`): a file that is neither a month nor `sem-data.csv`, a header that lacks the title or the day or differs from the others, a row in another file than its day's month, a hidden row without a full date outside `sem-data.csv`, a visible row without a title or a full date, an agenda's unreadable `Dia final`. `--fix` moves misplaced rows to their files as they are written (the same bytes), each before the first row dated later, creating a month's file with the shared header and removing a file the moves leave empty. The split of the old `data/agenda.csv` (2026-11-05) was made this way: the same rows, activities, ids and `agenda.ics`.
- Adding a folder of month files (another list that grows by date): a builder in `js/` that turns a month's rows into `{ events, invalid, warnings, missing }`, each event with `date`, `dateKey` and `line` (and `endDateKey` for a period), and a `columns(headers)` map with at least `title`, `day` and `visible` (as `js/news.js`); its `*_DIR` in `js/config.js`, added to `MONTH_FOLDERS`; its entry in `FOLDERS` (`tools/folders.mjs`: path, builder, columns, the day column's name for messages); and its page's reader, `monthlySource(DIR, build)` (`js/page.js`). The hook (which asks `month-files.mjs --list` for the indexes), the checks, `--fix`, the index and the offline worker (every `MONTH_FOLDERS` entry is a `dir` of `sw.js`) then cover it; `month-files.mjs` refuses a folder listed in one place and not the other.
- Monthly, not weekly: the month view is a month, a month's file stays small (October 2026, the agenda's busiest so far: 28 rows, 25 KB, 9 KB gzipped) and easy to find by hand, and a month has no ambiguous edges (weeks cross months and years). If a month ever grows too big to read at once, the index is where to split it further.
- No Google Sheets (decided 2026-11-05): the agenda, the news and Comércio are CSV files edited in this repository and published with a push; nothing reads a published sheet.

Comércio reads `COMMERCE_CSV_URL` (`data/comercio.csv`: the form's answers, copied in by the team), same formats. The columns are the Google Form's questions as they are, matched by a word they contain (`COLUMNS` in `js/businesses.js`, checked in order: consent before contact before other networks, so "Como podemos entrar em contato?" and "Podemos incluir … com a rede?" land right): `Carimbo de data/hora` (the date of the answer: the business's review while `Última revisão` is empty), `Nome da empresa ou negócio` (required), `Cidade e estado`, `Qual o Instagram da empresa?`, `O que sua empresa oferece?`, `Como podemos entrar em contato?`, `Sua empresa tem site ou outra rede social?`, `Podemos incluir sua empresa na nossa planilha e compartilhar suas informações com a rede?` (only a yes is shown, `isChecked`; a sheet without the column shows every row), plus columns the NOS team adds to the right: `Categoria` (free text; the order of the chips is `COMMERCE_CATEGORIES`, then alphabetical), `Endereço` (a street address the team reads out of the answers: an "Endereço" row linking to a Google Maps search in the business's city, and the 📍 line of the message), `Última revisão` (dd/mm/aaaa: the day someone confirmed the business's details with it; see "Review dates" below), `Visível` (`isHidden`) and `Notas internas` (ignored). `buildBusinesses` returns `{ businesses, invalid, stale, missing }` sorted by name (pt-BR collation); rows without a name or without any date are reported (`invalid`, `reason` `'name'` or `'review'`), rows not reviewed for `COMMERCE_REVIEW_MONTHS` are left out and listed in `stale` (Comércio prints them in the console for the team, not to visitors), rows without consent or hidden are skipped silently. What it reads out of the free text, and nothing else: the name and a subtitle (" - " or a trailing "(…)"), the letter (`#` for digits and signs), an all-caps offer in sentence case, the city (`HOME_CITY` with or without the state → `''`; others title-cased, another state as "City/UF", several cities joined with "e", a state alone kept as "MS"), Brazilian phone numbers with their area code in the contact answer (11 digits with a 9 → mobile: WhatsApp link and a call glyph; 10 → landline; numbers without the area code stay text), the Instagram handle (a link, "@handle" or a bare handle, accents stripped, a bare "Sim"/"Instagram" refused), the links of the other-networks answer named by domain (Google, Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp, else "Site"), and the answers' own words as notes when they add something (`contactNote` when no number was found, `otherNote` unless it only names channels already shown or says no). Ids are `biz-<slug of the name>`; `search` is the accent-free text every typed word must appear in. `data/comercio.csv` holds the answers of 2026-10-26 to 2026-10-28 verbatim, with `Categoria` (and `Endereço`) filled by hand, except where a person limited what may be shared: the file is public, so those contacts are removed from it (never only hidden), the consent stays "Sim" when they agreed to appear, and `Notas internas` says what was removed and why (Lilith Art & Tattoo: Instagram only; Patycakes: no contacts).

The Mapa reads one CSV per layer, `MAP_LAYERS` in `js/config.js` (`data/mapa/<id>.csv`; each layer with its `id`, `name`, `one` (a place of it: "Feira"), `file`, `icon` (a drawing of `js/icons.js`, the same on its points, its chip and its door glyph) and an optional `credit`, who surveys and checks it). Same formats as the other sheets. Columns (matched without accents, case or spacing by `ALIASES` in `js/places.js`): `Nome` and `Coordenadas` (required: "lat, lon", exact; a street address alone lands too far off), `Lugar`, `Endereço`, `Bairro`, `Quando` (several separated by `;`; times tidied to the agenda's way, "19h30 às 21h", by `tidyTimes`), `Descrição`, `Contato` (phones read as Comércio's: mobiles → WhatsApp, landlines → call, others kept as typed), `Instagram`, `Link`, `Fonte` (the source post), `Última revisão` (required), `Visível` and `Notas internas`. `capoeira.csv` is the Mapa da Capoeira's sheet of COMAFRO Dourados verbatim (copied 2026-11-11; `mapacapoeira.org` read it as a published Google Sheet), read through aliases: `Grupo`, `Nome do Endereço`, `Nome do Responsável`, `Horários de Treino`, `Telefone de Contato`, `Plus Code` (names the bairro when the address doesn't), `Última revisão em`, `Mostrar no Mapa`. `buildPlaces(layer, headers, records, lines)` returns `{ places, invalid, stale, missing }`: hidden rows skipped silently; rows without a name, coordinates or a review date reported (the page's notice lists them); rows not reviewed for `MAP_REVIEW_MONTHS` left out and listed in `stale` (console). Ids are `<layer>-<slug of the name and the place>` (the name once when the place starts with it), `-2`… for repeats: `mapa.html#<id>` opens the place. Accents lost in typing are put back as the sheets are read (`SPELLING` in `js/places.js`: whole words, "Jardim Italia" → "Jardim Itália"), so COMAFRO's sheet stays as they keep it; tell COMAFRO about each one. A place can be held off the map by its id (`MAP_HELD` in `js/config.js`, with the reason; listed in the console as `held`), without touching its sheet; it is empty since 2026-11-11. The `Instagram` column is the place's own account; another organization's (a students' union on a university) goes only in `Fonte`. The other layers' rows came from the survey (`../coleta-instagram/`) with coordinates from IBGE's CNEFE 2022 address file (`../scripts/coordenadas.py`) or OpenStreetMap; rows whose place or coordinates need confirming stay hidden with the reason in `Notas internas`. Aldeias, retomadas, terreiros, the quilombo, camps and homes go on the map only by the NOS team's decision (on 2026-11-11 it kept the AJI's capoeira training in the Aldeia Bororó and the Templo Girassol).

### Review dates

Comércio and the Mapa show only rows reviewed recently (`js/review.js`): a business or a place nobody has confirmed with its source for `COMMERCE_REVIEW_MONTHS` / `MAP_REVIEW_MONTHS` (12 each, in `js/config.js`) leaves the site by itself, on the day after its deadline (`reviewDeadline`: the review date plus the months), until someone confirms it and writes the new date in `Última revisão`. A year because businesses, groups and their schedules follow the year's cycle, and COMAFRO's reviews are spread over it (six months would already drop six of its 18 places). The agenda and the news need none: their rows age by their own dates. The rows left out are listed in the browser console (`console.info`) for the team; visitors see nothing.

## Instagram scan

`tools/instagram/` is where the agenda and the news get their material. `pages.json` is the list of tracked pages, a static list: every organization of the survey (`../coleta-instagram/`) with an Instagram page, whether or not it has fed the agenda or the news yet, plus the ones the team adds. `scan.py` (Python standard library, `playwright-cli`, `tesseract`) reads them, and what it read goes to `tools/instagram/dumps/<date-time>/`, ignored by git. The user runs it alone, `python3 tools/instagram/scan.py` (handles as arguments read only those pages); an agent reads the dump afterwards.

- `pages.json`: `settings` (`maxPostsPerPage`; `newPageDays`, the window of a page's first read) and `pages`, one per line: `handle`, `name` (the value its rows use in `Organização`), `sector` (`Setor`), and `lastScan` and `lastPost` (the code of the newest post the scan saw), which the script writes as soon as each page is done, so a stopped run resumes. `save_config` keeps any other field a page has, so new per-page settings need no change there.
- Only new posts get opened. Everything under `lastPost` in the grid is old. Without it, a post's link tells the day it was created (the shortcode holds it, `coded_time`), so older tiles and old pinned posts stay closed; the first older tile is opened once, because a post prepared ahead shows a later date than its code (the test behind this is in `../COLETA.md`, § 3.7).
- It has its own browser session and profile (`SESSION`, `BROWSER_PROFILE`, in `~/.local/share/nos-dourados/instagram-browser`): `playwright-cli`'s default session is shared with every project on the machine, where another tool can close or navigate the window.
- A dump holds `README.md` (the pages, how many new posts each, problems), one `<handle>.md` per page (its bio, then each new post: date and link, collaborators, tagged place, Instagram's event reminder, `Imagem N/M` paths, the caption, the OCR text) and `img/`. Every carousel slide and reel cover is saved and read; reels' audio and video frames are not.
- From a dump to rows: read the new posts of the newest folder(s). OCR is a lead, not a source: open the image with Read to confirm a date, time or place before it goes into a row. Future activities go in the agenda's month files (`Fonte` the post, `Notas internas` where each detail came from; `Visível` FALSE for public-service notices, undated drafts and events elsewhere with no organization from Dourados; members-only activities left out), and news candidates go in `noticias-candidatas.md` in the dump's folder for the team to choose (a story's rules are under "Editando as notícias" in `README.md`). Commit, but ask before pushing: pushing `main` deploys. Commit `pages.json` with the rows.
- `scan.py` also holds the Instagram reading (`*_JS`, `profile`, `fetch_posts`, `posts_md`, OCR) that `../scripts/ig.py`, the survey tool outside this repository, imports for its own commands: probe a live page with `playwright-cli eval` before changing a selector.

## Architecture

Agenda data flow: `app.js` reads the agenda's months through `month-files.js` (`agendaSource()` in `page.js`: the index, then the months its window needs) → `csv.js` `parseCSV` → `events.js` `buildEvents` (per month, merged by `mergeEvents`) → views (`agenda.js`, `month.js`) → clicking an activity fills the panel ("bench") from `app.js`, with `whatsapp.js` (message + `wa.me` link + preview) and `flyer.js` (flyer DOM + PNG export); the calendar circles and pills open `save-menu.js` (the options), which takes the calendar files and links from `ics.js` (also used by `tools/agenda-ics.mjs` for the published feed). Notícias: `noticias.js` → `newsSource()` (the same reader, with `news.js` `buildNews`) → the list and the panel, the stories' agenda links read from the agenda's month files (`linkActivities`), the message from `whatsapp.js` `buildNewsMessage`. Comércio: `comercio.js` → `page.js` `readSheet` → `businesses.js` `buildBusinesses` → the list. Mapa: `mapa.js` → `readSheet` per layer (`Promise.allSettled`: a layer that fails says so and the others show) → `places.js` `buildPlaces` → the map (MapLibre, global `maplibregl` from `js/vendor/`, the style from `map-style.js`), the chips, the panel's list and the card, with `whatsapp.js` `buildPlaceMessage`/`directionsUrl`. Início: `home.js` reads the agenda's months with something from today on, for Notícias' row (`renderNewsRow()`) the last two months of news, for the Mapa's row (`renderMapRow()`) the layers' sheets, and for Comércio's row (`renderCommerceRow()`) the businesses' sheet.

- On the desk (1100px) the agenda's column puts the organization filter beside Lista | Mês and the group chips under both, wrapping from the left edge (`.filters` steps aside with `display: contents`; it has no role of its own). The activity open in the panel takes a quiet soft-ink ring.
- `js/app.js` is the only stateful module of the agenda: one `state` object (view, group filter set and organization filter as `matchKey` values, selected activity, past toggle, month/year, selected day, the month grid's keyboard position) and all DOM wiring. The window of months read lives in `agenda` (`js/month-files.js`); going back in time goes through `goBack(day)`, whose reading state ('loading', 'failed') `backJobs` keeps per first day until it is asked for again; `useAgenda()` puts what was read on screen (`state.events`, colours, the top line, the notices). Other modules are render/format helpers that take data and return DOM nodes or strings. Rendering is full re-render via `replaceChildren()`; no framework.
- Focus across re-renders: every rebuildable focusable node carries `data-focus` (`chip:<key>`, `line:<id>` on the activity cards, `day:<key>`, `month:prev|next|today|title`, `past-toggle`, `clear-filters`); `rerender()` puts focus back on the new twin (with fallbacks in `FOCUS_FALLBACK`). Screen-reader announcements go through the persistent `#live` region (`announce()`), never through re-rendered nodes.
- "Now" is not frozen: `today`/`todayKey` are updated by `tick()` (every minute, on `visibilitychange`/`pageshow`); the view and the top line re-render when the day turns or the next activity changes, and the agenda (the index and the months read so far) is read again after 30+ minutes hidden (`reload()`, keeping filters and selection while they still exist, and trying again a month that couldn't be read; if the reading fails, what is on screen stays, without an error). The home does the same for its greeting (the hour), its date and its card (`tick()`, `load()` in `js/home.js`); `js/sky.js` keeps the sky's hour on every page.
- Event object shape is defined in `buildEvents`. Ids are stable across re-fetches: `ev-<dateKey>-<slug of the title>` (`-2`, `-3`… for repeats), so rows inserted above don't swap the open activity for another. Dates are local `Date` objects, never UTC; `dateKey` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) is compared as a string. Multi-day activities have `endDate`/`endDateKey`; helpers `lastDayKey`, `dayKeys`. Times are minutes since midnight (`time`, `endTime`) next to the raw strings; formatters fall back to the raw text. `matchKey` (punctuation-free) matches people-typed names (sectors, organizations, cities).
- List (`agenda.js`): the days on the land (`section.day-group`, its `h3.day-head` "Hoje · terça, 27/10" with Hoje underlined by hand, "Sábado, 31/10", a holiday's name and a "Feriado" tag; the full date for screen readers), each with its activities as cards (`div.event-card`, `renderCard`: the title and a share mark, then the time, the organization with its group's dot and its full name where known (else its group), the place, and a multi-day period). Today is always listed ("Nada marcado para hoje" when empty); holidays between the first and the last listed day are listed as day lines. Past days are hidden until "Ver N atividades anteriores" (N counts the past read so far: from the 1st of last month, or further back once read); with the past shown, `button.past-more` "Ver atividades de setembro" heads the days and reads the month before (`loadOlder`; "Carregando as atividades de setembro…" while it comes, aria-disabled so focus stays; a failure with "Tentar de novo"; the count it added is announced), and with nothing read behind today but older months in the index the toggle says "Ver atividades anteriores" and reads the month before on its own; a multi-day activity that started before today is listed under today ("Em andamento, até …", `groupByShownDay`); each day lists its own activities by time, then the running ones (`dayOrder`). With filters on and nothing ahead, a quiet block with "Ver atividades anteriores" / "Limpar filtros". The type stays in the panel and the message, not on the cards. The card's button is its title (`button.event-open`, `data-focus="line:<id>"`), stretched over the whole card by its `::after` (which also draws the focus ring around the card); `aria-describedby` reads the "Cancelada" tag, the reason and the meta rows after the title. Beside the title, `span.event-actions` holds the calendar circle (`button.save-circle`, `data-focus="save:<id>"`, its own button laid over the stretched one, only while the activity hasn't ended and isn't cancelled) and the decorative share mark (`aria-hidden`; a tap on it opens the panel, as anywhere on the card). Under 359px the share mark goes, the calendar circle stays.
- Month (`month.js`): Sunday-first, built as week rows inside a card; Sundays and holidays are shaded as days off (`is-off`), today's numeral is underlined by hand, the next activity's day circled, the chosen day ringed in soft ink (a quiet mark: the strong ring is focus's). On phones one-day activities are their groups' dots and multi-day ones thin bars in lanes along the foot of the week (`weekBars`, each day reserving only the lanes that cross it); from 1280px each day lists its entries (dot, time, title, three lines at most) and the bars are tinted with the group's colour, its dot and the title in ink. The grid is one tab stop with arrow/Home/End/PageUp/PageDown navigation (`onMove`). The chosen day is listed under the month (`renderDay`, only while that day is on the month's page). A month before the window is read when it is turned to (`monthReadiness`, from `renderView`): while its own days wait, the grid is `aria-busy` and a line under it says "Carregando as atividades de setembro…" (or, unread, that it couldn't, with "Tentar de novo", `retryMonth`); the first week's grey days of the month before fill in when they come, and the month's first day with activities is picked when it arrives; from 1280px a holiday's name sits on its own line under the numeral and its week (`has-holiday`) makes room for two lines, so the bars stay level; turning the month picks today or the month's first day with activities; picking a grey day flips to its month.
- Days off: Sundays and national holidays (`holidays.js`: fixed dates + Good Friday from Easter; `EXTRA_HOLIDAYS` in config for local ones — none confirmed yet). `isRedLetter` names them in code (the month flyer prints them in the sky's soft ink).
- The next activity (`findNext` in `app.js`, computed on the filtered list, through `findNextEvent`/`upcomingEvents` in `events.js`, which the home shares): first non-cancelled activity starting today-or-later (today's count until an hour after they start; periods already running are not "next"). Its time is circled by hand in urucum with "a próxima" on its start day's card (`handMark` in `js/icons.js`), its day circled in the month, and its panel says "a próxima"; on the home its date is circled.

Head: the day in Bricolage ("Sábado, 31 de outubro"; a period as "Sex, 09/10 a Dom, 11/10"), the time or "Horário a confirmar" (nothing for a deadline or a period, `awaitsTime`), "a próxima"; struck through when cancelled; under them "Salvar na minha agenda" (`#save-pill` in `div.bench-save`, its own line of the wrapping head; hidden for cancelled or ended activities). Facts list (organization with its dot and full names from `ORG_NAMES`, period, place, type, contact, link, source), description, Mensagem/Panfleto tabs (`showPanel`, which sets `data-panel`; the facts and the description stay above the tabs on both, one copy that doesn't depend on the tab, so on Panfleto the flyer is one scroll down; an activity opens at the panel's top (`settleScroll`), and picking a tab scrolls down until its content starts under the stuck tab bar, never up (`revealPanel`)), sticky action bar. Phone numbers in `Contato` become links (`contactNode`: mobiles → `wa.me`, landlines → `tel:`); `Link`/`Fonte` go through `safeUrl` (only http(s); bare domains get `https://`; anything else is shown as text). Cancelled: a "Cancelada" tag in ink, the reason, the title struck.

The summary of the activity heads the options except on the desk when they come from the panel's own pill. No site can see the person's calendar: nothing here checks what they already saved, and nothing confirms they saved (they confirm in their calendar).

### Light, dark and the sky

- Colours: every colour is a custom property in `css/theme.css`, in two layers — palette (`--color-*`: the raw colours, named for what they are, light and dark both: clay, terra roxa, urucum, ipê-rosa, the sky's bands at each hour, the groups by day and at night) and roles (`--role-*`: what each colour is for). `style.css` and `flyers.css` use roles only; the only literals are `transparent`, `currentColor`, the band's image (`assets/faixa.svg`) and, inside `@media (forced-colors: active)`, system colours. The palette never changes with the theme; the roles do.
- Light and dark: the roles on `:root` are the light theme; `:root[data-scheme='dark']` swaps them (terra roxa ground, moonlit ink, the night's urucum, the groups' lighter colours). The inline script in each page's head sets `data-scheme` before the first paint: the visitor's choice (the theme button: `localStorage` `nos.tema` = `claro`/`escuro`; choosing what the system says forgets it), else `prefers-color-scheme`. `?tema=claro` or `?tema=escuro` forces one for the visit (screenshots, links; not saved). The old named themes (`data-theme`, `?tema=<name>`, the `exemplo` theme) are gone: a variant now means a light and a dark set and the sky's hours, so it is a new pair of role blocks in `theme.css`.
- The sky by the hour: `data-sky` on `<html>` (`dawn` amanhecer 5–9h, `day` dia 9–16h, `dusk` entardecer 16–19h, `night` noite 19–5h, by the local clock; `?ceu=amanhecer|dia|entardecer|noite` forces one). Each period sets `--role-sky-1…5` (flat bands from the top down to the horizon), the sky's ink and soft ink (≥4.5:1 on every band), the hand that draws the astro and the astro itself (the sun; the moon and stars at night). The dark theme's sky is always the night. `js/sky.js` keeps `data-scheme` and `data-sky` right (every minute, visibility, the system's change, another tab's `storage` event), sets the button's `aria-pressed`, and copies `--role-sky-1` into both `theme-color` metas (one per system scheme, static values `--color-day-1` and `--color-night-1`).

## PWA and offline

- `sw.js` is network-first for every same-origin GET and for the sheets (Comércio's CSV may be on another origin): the live site and sheets always win and the cache is only the fallback, so there is no version to bump on deploy. `SHELL` is precached on install, because the first visit isn't controlled yet and only SHELL gets saved then: keep it in step with the five pages and every file they load (stylesheets, fonts, the band `assets/faixa.svg`, the module graphs from `js/home.js`, `js/app.js`, `js/noticias.js`, `js/mapa.js` and `js/comercio.js`, the vendored image library, the manifest icons, the favicons); the screenshots and `og-image.png` stay out, and so does MapLibre (`js/vendor/maplibre-gl.js` and `.css`, about 210 KB compressed: only the Mapa uses it, and offline it would draw nothing, the tiles coming from OpenFreeMap; offline the Mapa shows its list). The install also saves Comércio's CSV, the Mapa's sheets and, for each folder of month files, its `index.json` and the months whose `until` reaches the 1st of the month before last (`saveMonths`: what the news page opens with, `openingDay` in `noticias.js`; the agenda and the home need less). Copies are keyed by absolute URL, pages without their query (so `?tema=` or `?utm_…` links don't multiply). Saved sheet copies get an `X-Cached-At` stamp (exposed to the page for a cross-origin CSV), which makes the agenda show the "Sem conexão" notice (`agenda.savedAt()`, the oldest copy read). The cache is `nos-dourados-v1`; activation deletes only older `nos-dourados-*` caches, so other apps on the same origin (GitHub Pages) keep theirs. `COMMERCE_CSV_URL` and each layer's file of `MAP_LAYERS` reach the worker as `csv` and each folder of `MONTH_FOLDERS` as `dir`, a folder whose every file is a sheet (`sw.js?csv=…&dir=data%2Fagenda%2F&dir=data%2Fnoticias%2F`, `registerWorker` in `js/page.js`, the same address from every page or each would replace the others' worker), so changing either registers a new worker. Offline, a month never read before can't be shown: the month view, "Ver atividades de…" and "Ver notícias de…" say so, with "Tentar de novo".
- `index.html` and its folder share one saved copy; offline, only addresses of the app's own folder fall back to the saved home page (deeper ones would load it at the wrong base, unstyled). Activation also prunes the cache to SHELL plus the sheets (Comércio's CSV and every file of the folders of month files read so far), so removed files and an old spreadsheet's copy (the old `data/agenda.csv`) don't linger.
- Two defensive paths, kept from when a published Google Sheet was planned (dropped on 2026-11-05) and harmless where they aren't needed: a response that followed a redirect (a published Google Sheets CSV answers 307 to googleusercontent.com) goes back to the page as a plain copy, because Playwright's Firefox build never delivers a redirected response from `respondWith` (the page's fetch hangs; stock Firefox 156 is fine); and `fetchSheet` in `js/page.js` falls back to the saved copy (`caches.match`) when the fetch throws, because Firefox's offline mode as emulated in tests doesn't ask the worker for `no-store` requests.
- `manifest.webmanifest`: id `/nos-dourados` (never change it: a new id is a new app; `./` would resolve to the origin root on a sub-path host such as GitHub Pages), name and short_name "NOS Dourados", `start_url` `./` (the home), `shortcuts` to Agenda, Notícias, Mapa and Comércio, `theme_color` #6FAEE3 (the day sky's top band, the static light `theme-color`; neither can read CSS variables), `background_color` #F2E3D7 (the clay), no orientation lock (WCAG 1.3.4), four narrow screenshots (three of the agenda, one of Comércio) and one wide one.
- The head has PNG favicons (16/32/48), the apple-touch-icon, `apple-mobile-web-app-*` (status bar "black": "black-translucent" would put the white clock over the light skies, and needs a test on a real iPhone) and the og/twitter tags. `og:url`, `og:image` and `canonical` use the absolute https address, because crawlers don't resolve relative URLs; `og:image` carries `?v=4` (bump it when the image changes).
- Assets are rendered from HTML by `node assets/render.cjs [icons|og|shots]`, which serves the folder itself and stamps each PNG with its origin (a `tEXt` chunk under the impeccable skill's keyword: `impeccable embed-prompt <file> --read` shows it, `--scan assets` lists files without one) (Playwright: `PLAYWRIGHT_CORE`/`CHROMIUM` env vars, a local `playwright-core` install, or the maintainer's paths; a refused capture is tried once more). `assets/icon.html` (variants `#any`, `#maskable`, `#apple`, `#favicon`, drawn at the viewport size) prints the NOS mark (`MARK` in `js/icons.js`) on the dawn sky over the band and the clay, its O the sun inside the urucum loop; the favicons are that sun in its loop alone on the sky, the loop drawn as a stroke from its centre line kept in `icon.html`. `assets/og-image.html` (the link preview) is the home's first view at poster scale: the dawn sky, the mark, "Dourados" and the full name of `js/config.js` at the left, the big sun rising behind the band at the right; its type is sized for the ~330px-wide WhatsApp bubble. Both set their own `data-scheme="light"` and `data-sky="dawn"`, use the theme roles and set `html[data-ready]` when done. The screenshots are the site itself (`agenda.html`, `comercio.html`) in the light theme on a fixed day (2026-10-15, 10h, the day sky).
- Landscape is allowed. With `viewport-fit=cover`, the header, `.shell`, the home, the footer, the bar at the foot, the full-width phone sheet and the skip link pad with `env(safe-area-inset-*)`; on phones on their side (`max-height: 500px` and landscape) the sheet takes nearly the whole height, and in flyer mode the preview stands beside the format bar and the style picker.

## Git workflow

One developer and little parallel work, so keep git light:

- No pull requests. Work lands on `main` directly.
- Worktrees (`.claude/worktrees/`, git-ignored) are fine for isolating a task, but when it is done, fast-forward `main` to the worktree branch (`git merge --ff-only <branch>` in the main checkout; rebase the branch on `main` first if `main` moved), push `main`, then delete the branch (local and remote) and remove the worktree. Nothing stays parked on a side branch.
- Commit once per significant chunk of work (one feature, fix or doc change per commit) rather than one big commit, so each commit is easy to review on its own.
- Pushing `main` publishes the site (GitHub Pages builds from `main` / root), so check the change works before pushing.

### The generated files

Each folder of month files' `index.json` (`data/agenda/`, `data/noticias/`; by `tools/month-files.mjs`) and `agenda.ics` (`FEED_FILE`, by `tools/agenda-ics.mjs`, from every month of the agenda) are generated and committed: never edit them by hand. `tools/folders.mjs` reads the folders for both, with the site's own modules (`FOLDERS`: each folder's path, builder, columns and day column).

- `.githooks/pre-commit` runs both on every commit, on the staged files (the staged `data/`, `js/` and `tools/`, never unstaged edits), and adds what they wrote to the commit, so a change to a month's file, its index and the feed always land together; a commit that doesn't change them leaves them untouched (same data, same bytes). The indexes it adds are the ones `node tools/month-files.mjs --list` names (as staged). It refuses the commit, with the reason, when `month-files.mjs` finds a problem (a row in another file than its day's month, a stray file, a different header, a row the site couldn't read, a folder in `MONTH_FOLDERS` without its entry in `tools/folders.mjs` or the reverse; see "Month files": `node tools/month-files.mjs --fix` moves rows to their files), when the feed can't be built, or when Node 22+ is missing. `.githooks/post-commit` puts the committed generated files back in git's index after `git commit <file>` (git commits a temporary index then).
- Git runs them once the clone says `git config core.hooksPath .githooks` (set in this clone; worktrees share it; a fresh clone needs it once). Check with `git config --get core.hooksPath`.
- `node tools/month-files.mjs --check` and `node tools/agenda-ics.mjs --check` say whether the committed files are current (exit 1 if not); without `--check` they rebuild them by hand. `.gitattributes` keeps the feed's CRLF line endings (RFC 5545) and marks both as generated.
- On a merge or rebase conflict in `index.json` or `agenda.ics`, don't merge their text: resolve the month files, rebuild with `node tools/month-files.mjs && node tools/agenda-ics.mjs`, add them.
