# Design System: NOS Dourados

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Céu · Terra"**

NOS opens on the sky over Dourados at this hour and the land beneath it. Every page stacks the same three layers: the header is the sky of this hour, painted in five flat bands (amanhecer, dia, entardecer and noite by the clock in the light theme, always the night in the dark theme); the woven band under it is the horizon; below lies the land, light clay by day and terra roxa at night, where the activities, the doors and the businesses rest as clear rounded cards; on the Mapa the land is the city itself, drawn in the same clay and terra roxa. The sun, a disc inside an urucum loop drawn by hand, rises big from behind the band and runs off the edge; at night a small moon, about a fifth of the sun, sits high among the stars.

The hand is the system's signature. The same urucum pen that draws the sun's loop and the O of the NOS mark circles the next activity and underlines today: urucum is the one marking colour, and it marks rather than fills. Each page wears one colour of the landscape on its door and its own panel (Agenda urucum, Notícias ipê-amarelo, Mapa erva-mate, Comércio ipê-rosa); the organizations' eight groups are coloured dots, and the Mapa's layers are drawings, never colours. Bricolage Grotesque is NOS's voice (the greeting, dates, page titles, day heads) and Inter the reading text and the controls. The density is calm and phone-first: lists of cards with 44px targets on the phone, a production bench on the desk, never a dashboard.

The world refuses the hero banner over icon tiles, the dashboard, the grey list with thin dividers and coloured pills, the marketplace (carousels, banners, prices, ratings), the stock web map (its own colours, coloured category pins, popups), and the Folhinha's notepad and torn-off sheets; nothing of the old campaign interface (the metro line to election day, the countdown, the slogan) comes back, and nothing should read as a startup, a city hall or an election campaign. Light and dark are both finished themes. The approved artboards (`design/direcao-ceu-terra-*.html`) also draw sections and a search that were deliberately not built; where they and the build diverge, the build is the reference. The flyers carry the same world into a picture: the sky of the activity's hour over the band and the land (see Components).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Sky over land on every page, meeting at a woven horizon.
- The sky in flat bands by the hour: hard stops, never a soft gradient.
- One marking colour, urucum, drawn by hand: loops and strokes, not badges.
- Pages in colours of the landscape; the organizations' groups as dots, the Mapa's layers as drawings.
- Bricolage Grotesque 800 for the voice, Inter for reading; tabular dates.
- Rounded cards and standard controls: pills, circles, 18px cards.
- A soft earth-tinted shadow by day, a 1px line at night.
- One authored motion: the home's sun rises.

## Colors

A palette taken from the place: clay and terra roxa for the land, urucum, the ipês and erva-mate from its plants, the sky of Dourados at four hours, and the city's map painted in them. Every colour is a palette value in `css/theme.css` (`--color-*`, named for what it is) and reaches the pages only through a role (`--role-*`, named for what it does), the map included (`js/map-style.js` reads its roles into MapLibre); the light and dark themes and the hour's sky swap the roles, never the palette.

- **Urucum** (#C2410C): the one marking colour. It fills the primary button (white text, 5.18:1) and strokes the hand marks on the land: the loop around the next activity, the stroke under today. ### Named Rules
**The One Marking Colour Rule.** Urucum marks; it never fills an area. At full strength it is only the hand (the loop around the next activity, the stroke under today, the sun's loop, the mark's O), the primary button, the open page and, on the Mapa, the person's own position; its tints may colour the Agenda's door, the phone bar's pill, selection, the error notice and that position's halo. If urucum covers a panel, a header or a card on the site, it is wrong.

**The Flat Sky Rule.** The sky is five flat bands with hard stops, coloured by the hour in the light theme and always the night in the dark theme: never a soft gradient, never a photo. Text on it uses only the sky's ink and soft ink, which hold 4.5:1 or more on every band of every hour (the lowest pair is 5.05:1, the dusk's soft ink); urucum on the sky is a drawn mark, never the only sign of a state.

**The Dots, Never Shapes Rule.** A group is a coloured dot (8px; 6px in the phone month), a thin bar in the phone month, or a tinted bar with its dot on the desk month: never a shape, an icon, a filled card or a coloured pill, and never the only place the group is named (the chips and the panel say it in words). A cancelled activity keeps its group's colour as an outline.

**The Plain Paint Rule.** MapLibre reads no CSS: the fourteen roles `js/map-style.js` reads (`--role-map-land` to `--role-map-indigena-line`) each resolve to a plain palette colour, never a `color-mix()`, so a mix the map needs is worked out into the palette (Map Green is the clay with 15% of verde, Map Indígena Line the soft ink at 45% on the clay). What the page draws over the map (the points, the drop, the small print's veil: `--role-pin-shadow`, `--role-pin-drop`, `--role-map-veil`) is CSS and mixes like the rest of the site.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bricolage Grotesque (variable, 500–800, with its optical-size axis; falls back to Inter, system-ui, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Inter (variable, 400–700; falls back to system-ui, sans-serif)
**On the flyers:** the same two, Bricolage Grotesque (700, 800) with its optical size pinned at 96 and Inter (500 to 700), in px on a fixed canvas
**On the map:** the tiles' own labels (bairros, roads, streets, water, the Reserva's name) in Noto Sans Regular and Italic, the glyphs MapLibre takes from OpenFreeMap with the tiles (`MAP_GLYPHS`); everything the page sets over the map is in the two faces above

**Character:** Bricolage Grotesque is the voice and Inter the reading text. ### Named Rules
**The Two Voices Rule.** Bricolage Grotesque is NOS speaking: the greeting, dates, page titles, day heads, the headings that file things (doors, letters, businesses, places) and the news headlines, which the team writes (750 on the cards, 800 for the lead and in the panel). Inter is what the organizations announce and every control: an activity's title is the organization's own words and is never set in Bricolage, and in a story what an organization said stays in Inter, attributed.

**The Tabular Dates Rule.** Every date, time and count is set with tabular figures, so days and hours line up down a list.

**The Planned Flyer Rule.** A flyer sets only the faces, weights and optical size its layout engine measured (`.impeccable/flyer-metrics.html`), and every line is planned before it is drawn: the preview and the PNG are one picture. Its numerals stand alone, so they keep the font's proportional figures; dates and times in lists keep the tabular ones. A new face or weight means measuring again.

## Elevation & Depth

A hybrid that changes with the light. By day, cards rest on the clay with a soft two-layer shadow tinted from the earth, never grey; at night the shadow goes and a 1px terra roxa line draws the card's edge, so the dark theme has no shadows on the land. Only the bottom sheet, lifted over its scrim, keeps a shadow at night. Flat colour panels (the doors, the Mapa's invitation) carry no shadow: the colour is the card. Over the map, whatever floats rests like a card (the chips, "Camadas", the map's buttons, the desk's rail and place card), and the points take a tighter shadow of their own so they stand off the streets. State shows as a ring, never a lift: nothing moves up on hover.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Day rest** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px color-mix(in srgb, #4A2212 8%, transparent), 0 6px 16px color-mix(in srgb, #4A2212 7%, transparent)`): activity cards, the month, the home's next card, the desk panel, business and information cards, the message bubble.
- **Night rest** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 0 transparent; border: 1px solid #4A2F28`): the same surfaces at night.
- **Sheet** (`box-shadow: 0 -8px 28px color-mix(in srgb, #4A2212 18%, transparent)`; at night `0 -8px 28px color-mix(in srgb, #0E0706 60%, transparent)`): the bottom sheet, over a scrim of the ink at 50% (Night Shade at 70%).
- **Popover** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 6px color-mix(in srgb, #4A2212 10%, transparent), 0 16px 40px color-mix(in srgb, #4A2212 18%, transparent)`; at night the 1px line and `0 16px 40px color-mix(in srgb, #0E0706 70%, transparent)`): the desk's calendar options, floating over the list or the panel (`--role-pop-shadow`).
- **Point** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px` in the card colour, then `0 1px 2px color-mix(in srgb, #4A2212 22%, transparent), 0 3px 8px color-mix(in srgb, #4A2212 16%, transparent)`; at night no shadow, the terra roxa ring alone drawing the edge): the map's points and counts (`--role-pin-shadow`; a count's double ring is `0 0 0 2.5px` in the ink and `0 0 0 5px` in the card colour).
- **Drop** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 2px 3px color-mix(in srgb, #4A2212 25%, transparent))`; none at night): the open place's drop (`--role-pin-drop`).
- **Selection ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1.5px` in the soft ink, added to the rest shadow; inset on the month's chosen day and in the Mapa's list): the activity open in the panel, the chosen day, the place open in the Mapa's list.
- **Focus ring** (`outline: 2px solid` Deep Urucum, `outline-offset: 2px`; Night Urucum in the dark theme; the sky's ink on the sky): every focusable element.

### Named Rules
**The Day Shadow, Night Line Rule.** Cards rest on the earth shadow by day and on a 1px line at night: never a shadow on the land in the dark theme, never a hard offset shadow, never a lift on hover.

**The Quiet Selection Rule.** Selection is a 1.5px soft-ink ring; focus is the strong 2px urucum ring (the sky's ink on the sky). They never trade places, and a selected card that takes focus shows both.

A layer is its drawing, never a colour. Places closer than a point's size merge, whatever their layers: two show both drawings overlapping by a quarter (−8px; −7px on phones), three or more become their count, a 34px disc of the card colour in its double ring, the number in Bricolage 800 (0.9375rem, tabular). A tap on a count brings its places apart or, when no zoom can (one address), opens the first in the layers' order. Every point, pair and count carries a transparent 44px tap circle, whatever its drawn size.
- **The drop:** the open place stands as a drop (42 by 50px) on its point, ink with a 2.5px ring of the card colour and its drawing at 20px, on the drop shadow by day; the places at the same address wait under it.
- **The place card:** a sheet over the map's lower half on phones (64% of the screen at most, with the grabber; Back closes it), centred at 560px from 600px, and a 400px card at the right on the desk. The head holds only the name (Bricolage 750, 1.375rem, balanced) and ✕. The line under the name carries the layer's drawing in a 24px disc, the layer at 600 in ink and who leads it in the soft ink ("Capoeira · Mestre Guerreiro"): never a label above the name. Then "Treinos" (for capoeira) or "Quando" in a 12px block of the fill (its head in Bricolage 750 at 1rem with a soft clock; its lines tabular, a group's name at 600 before its times), the description, the facts (where, the phones as WhatsApp or a call, Instagram, a link: the panel's 44px rows), "Também neste endereço" (the other places at the same address, 48px rows with their drawings and layers), and the review line in the soft ink at 0.8125rem ("Conferido pelo COMAFRO Dourados em 12/08/2026.", or "Revisado em …", then "Ver a fonte"). The action bar: "Enviar no WhatsApp" (primary) and "Como chegar" (outlined; a 48px circle on narrow screens).
- **The person:** "Onde estou" shows where the person is, on the device only: an 18px urucum disc ringed 3px in the card colour, in a 6px halo of the urucum tint.
- **Without the map:** when it can't open (no WebGL, MapLibre not loaded), the map goes and the panel's list is the page, on the land under a notice that says so: each layer a heading (its drawing, name and count) over its places, all open.

**The Inhabited Land Rule.** The tiles carry the Indigenous lands as nature reserves; the map draws them as inhabited land. The Reserva Indígena de Dourados (the Jaguapiru and Bororó villages, home to Kaiowá, Guarani Ñandeva and Terena people) is a land a little lighter than the ground, edged by a dashed line in the soft ink, with its name in the strong label: never the parks' green.

### Comércio
The list beside an information card whose icons are Deep Ipê-Rosa: business cards with the name in Bricolage 750, the offer, a quiet category tag, and labelled contact rows with the numbers at 700 in tabular figures.

### Icons
Lucide (ISC) drawings on a 24px grid with a 2px round stroke in `currentColor`, inline SVG: 16px in meta rows, 18px in buttons, chips and facts, 20px by default, 22px in the header, the phone bar and the doors. The Mapa's layers are drawings of the same set (Lucide's microphone, basket, masks, megaphone, cap and landmark, and a berimbau drawn in Lucide's grammar), each the same on its layer's points, chip, switch and door: 18px in the chips, 17px in the points (15px on phones), 20px in the drop, 16px in the 28px discs and 14px in the 24px one under a place's name. No glyphs or emoji as interface icons (the emoji in the WhatsApp message preview are the message's text, as it will be sent).

### The NOS Mark
NOS with the O circled by hand, sized by font-size (a 3.807em by 2.3em box): the letters take the current colour and the loop `--mark-loop`, which falls back to it; on the sky the letters take the sky's ink and the loop the sky's hand. The app icons print the mark on the dawn sky over the band and the clay; the favicons are the sun in its loop alone on the sky; the link preview is the home's first view at poster scale on the dawn sky. Another app name prints as the word in Bricolage 800 until a mark is drawn for it.

### Flyers
The 1080 by 1350 PNG flyers ("Folha do dia", "Agenda do mês", "Cartaz", and "Aviso de cancelamento", which every cancelled activity gets) are small landscapes of the same world, read first as a 300px thumbnail in a WhatsApp chat, then full screen. Each is the sky of the hour its activity starts (amanhecer, dia, entardecer, noite; the day's sky when there is no time) in five flat bands over the woven band (64px), and the land below: the light clay under the three light skies, terra roxa under the night, whatever the page's theme. The sky carries the flyer's idea in NOS's voice; the land carries the activity in Inter.
- **Folha do dia:** the day's numeral (Bricolage 800, up to 600px, -0.03em) stands 40px above the band in front of the rising sun, a disc inside the hand's loop running off the right edge (at night the small moon high above it and the stars). At its left, from its top line, the weekday (Bricolage 800) and the month (700, soft ink); on its baseline the time (800, its end apart in 700) or "Horário a confirmar" (nothing for a period of several days or a deadline: types Prazo and Campanha).
- **Agenda do mês:** the month's name (Bricolage 800, 104px) and its year, the weekday heads in Inter capitals, then the weeks as the sky's bands, from the first under the name to the last on the horizon. The activity's day is the sun or the moon inside the hand's loop, its numeral in ink; a period's other days are underlined by hand; Sundays and holidays take the soft ink.
- **Cartaz:** the title in Inter 700, as large as the sky allows (168px down to 56px; it gives up to 14% of that size for lines that end on no short word), hanging from the top with the sun rising in the sky left under it; at night it stands on the horizon under the moon. On the land, the date circled by hand in urucum with the time beside it.
- **Aviso de cancelamento:** "Cancelada" (Bricolage 800, up to 220px) on the sky of the activity's hour without its sun or moon (the stars stay at night), over the date and time the hand struck out. On the land, the title struck by an urucum stroke, "Motivo:" and the reason, then who and where in the soft ink, the group's dot as an outline.
- **The land, on every flyer:** the title (Inter 700, -0.02em); the organizations, the group's dot before each name in 700 with its full name in 500 and soft ink where it is known; the place after a pin (a globe online), with its city outside Dourados; a holiday's name; and the signature at the foot: the NOS mark, "Dourados", "Agenda das organizações" and the site's address. No type, logos or slogans: only the activity's own fields.

**The Sky of the Hour Rule.** A flyer's sky is the hour its activity starts, never the hour it is made, and the day's when there is no time. Its text uses the sky's inks only, and the bands stay sky behind it: no edge crosses a line of text or comes within 20px of it (display type from 100px, which the bands must cross, only keeps its cap line, x-height and foot clear), every band is at least 40px tall, and they grow up from the horizon where the text lets them.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every colour from a role (`--role-*` in `css/theme.css`); the only literals in the stylesheets are `transparent`, `currentColor`, the band's image and system colours under forced colours.
- **Do** paint the map only through its `--role-map-*` paint roles, each a plain palette colour (The Plain Paint Rule).
- **Do** finish every new surface in both themes and under all four skies, with any text on the sky in the sky's ink or soft ink (4.5:1 or more on every band).
- **Do** keep urucum for the hand, the primary button, the open page and the person's position on the map; give a new page its own colour of the landscape as a door tint, with its deep shade for icons and mixed text (Comércio ipê-rosa, Notícias ipê-amarelo, Mapa erva-mate).
- **Do** show a group as its dot beside its name, so colour is never the only carrier of meaning.
- **Do** circle the next activity and underline today with the hand marks, and nothing else on the site; on a flyer the hand circles the activity's date or day and strikes out what was cancelled.
- **Do** set dates in Bricolage 800 with tabular figures, headings at 700–750, and what the organizations announce in Inter.
- **Do** rest cards on the day shadow and the night line from the roles, and mark selection with the quiet soft-ink ring.
- **Do** keep every target at 44px or more (the 36px chips carry a 44px tap area, and every point, pair and count on the map a 44px tap circle) and size text in rem.
- **Do** credit the band as provisional in every footer while `assets/faixa.svg` is the provisional art.

### Don't:
- **Don't** fill a panel, a header or a card with full-strength urucum on the site, or use it for anything but marking.
- **Don't** draw the sky with a soft gradient, a photo or a texture, or set text on it in anything but the sky's inks.
- **Don't** give the groups shapes, icons, filled cards or coloured pills, and don't colour Comércio's categories or the Mapa's layers: a layer is its drawing on an ink point.
- **Don't** replace the band with an imitation or adaptation of any people's grafismo; the real band comes from artists of the Reserva Indígena de Dourados, signed, paid and credited.
- **Don't** put shadows on the land in the dark theme, use hard offset shadows, or lift elements on hover.
- **Don't** use the strong urucum ring for selection or the quiet ring for focus.
- **Don't** fetch fonts from Google Fonts, or set an activity's title in Bricolage, on the site or on a flyer.
- **Don't** add motion beyond the home's rising sun (1.6s, off under reduced motion); state changes are 0.15s colour transitions and the sheet's 0.3s slide. The header's fold is not motion of its own: it is the scroll, and the page's name fades over the first 16px of it, under reduced motion too. The Mapa's camera is the one exception, and it only answers a tap: it eases in 700ms to a place or to the person's position and fits a count's places in 600ms (MapLibre's own zoom buttons step in 500ms), all instant under reduced motion; its list's chevron turns in the same 0.15s as a state change.
- **Don't** bring the Folhinha's devices to the site (the red header card, bindings, perforations, a yellow "Próxima") or the old campaign interface (the metro line to election day, the countdown, the slogan); nothing should read as a startup, a city hall or an election campaign.
- **Don't** invent logos, photos, slogans, ratings or prices for the organizations or the businesses.
- **Don't** bring the stock web map to the Mapa (its own colours, coloured category pins, popups), or draw the Indigenous lands as parks (The Inhabited Land Rule).
