# Surface: Início (index.html)

Scope: the home of NOS Dourados: the greeting under the sky of this hour, the next activity resting on the horizon, and a door to each page that exists today (the agenda, calendario.html; Comércio, comercio.html, paused with "Em breve" since 2026-10-27). Visitor mode: **Operate** (a hub: pick where to go). Phone-first. Only current features: no doors, links or counts for sections that don't exist yet (Mutirões, Notícias, Mapa, Organizações come later).

Audience/job: someone opening nos.dourados.social (a shared link, the installed app) understands in one look that NOS holds the organizations' agenda (and soon the network's businesses), sees what comes next, sends it to WhatsApp, and reaches either page in one tap.

## Direction contract

THESIS: NOS opens on the sky over Dourados at this hour and the land beneath it: the woven band is the horizon, the urucum loop of the mark rises as the sun, and on the land below sit the next activity and the doors. Refuses the hero-banner-over-icon-tiles home, the dashboard, and the folhinha's notepad.

OWN-WORLD: Two layers, sky and land, meeting at the woven band. Sky: flat bands, no gradients — light theme follows the clock (amanhecer 5–9h in Terra's golden bands #93C1E6 #BED6E8 #F0D5C6 #F8C095 #FBD789, then dia, entardecer, noite derived in the same warm register), dark theme is always night (#140D26 #1C1233 #271740 #361C48 #47214A with small stars #F6E6C6). The sun (#F4A11E disc inside an urucum hand loop) rises big from behind the band and runs off the edge; the moon (#F4DDB0) is small (about a fifth of the sun) and sits high among the stars. The horizon is the provisional woven band (taquara twill with urucum selvage, never a copied grafismo), credited as provisional; the real band is made, signed and paid for by artists of the Reserva Indígena de Dourados (design/faixa-pesquisa.html). Land: light clay #F2E3D7 with cards #FFFBF7 and warm ink #221714 / soft #6A4E44 by day; terra roxa #231311 with cards #301B17, ink #F7EBE3 / soft #CFB5A9 at night. Urucum (#C2410C light, #FF8A5C dark) is the one marking colour: the hand loop circles the next activity, a hand stroke underlines today, the primary button, the open tab. Sections take landscape colours (Agenda urucum, Comércio ipê-rosa #C03A73); organization groups are coloured dots, never shapes. Bricolage Grotesque (800 greeting, dates, page titles; 700–750 headings) and Inter for reading; rounded cards, standard controls.

STORY: The visitor is greeted by the hour ("Bom dia, Dourados"), sees the next activity with its date circled by hand and sends it to WhatsApp, then opens the agenda or Comércio from its door.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone 390: status and top bar (NOS mark, "Dourados", the theme toggle) on the sky; the greeting (Bricolage 800, about 52px) and the date; the sun rising behind the 40px band at the right (at night a small moon high up, stars); the next-activity card resting on the band (date circled in urucum, "a próxima", title, organization with its dot, time or "horário a confirmar", place, share to WhatsApp); below, the doors in their landscape colours: Agenda (the count until the last date, a line of what is next) and Comércio ("Em breve" while paused); the band's provisional credit. Desktop 1440: a 276px sky with the top bar on it, the greeting at left and the big sun at right, the 44px band; below, the next activity with "Compartilhar no WhatsApp" and "Ver na agenda" plus "Também na agenda" (the next two or three), and the two doors. Calm, never a dashboard. Signature: the sun (or moon) over the woven horizon, the sky by the hour.

FORM: Céu · Terra, chosen by the user on 2026-10-27 after four rounds: round 1 roll (seed 5bf776bb, impeccable direction scope, Operate) → the user's steer "Padrão + Indigenous references" (D · Trama) → three variants (D3 · Céu chosen) → three Céu variants (Terra approved; Pipas kept for later). Approved artboards: design/direcao-ceu-terra-*.html (with the smaller, higher moon fixed on 2026-10-27).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
