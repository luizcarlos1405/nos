# Surface: Início (index.html)

Scope: the home of NOS Dourados: the city's question on the sky of this hour, the very next activity under the band, then what each page holds and who NOS follows. Visitor mode: **Persuade** since 2026-11-12 (Operate before): someone arriving from a shared link must understand in seconds what NOS is; the network still gets the next activity and every page in one tap. Phone-first. Only current features: the four pages that exist (Agenda, Notícias, Mapa, Comércio).

Audience/job: someone opening nos.dourados.social (a shared link, the installed app) reads that this is where to find what is happening in Dourados (the organizations' activities, their news, the places of culture and of the movements, the network's businesses), sees the very next activity with who organizes it, sends it to WhatsApp, and opens any page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home asks the city's question, "O que está acontecendo em Dourados?", on the sky of this hour, and answers it first with the very next activity, named as such, then with what each page holds today. Refuses the greeting-over-cards home it replaces (the four pastel doors), the hero banner over icon tiles, the dashboard, and any carousel or varal of activities.

OWN-WORLD: Céu · Terra as DESIGN.md records it. Sky: flat bands of this hour; the question in Bricolage 800 (sky ink), under it the line of what the site holds in the sky's soft ink; the lower bands and the sun (at night the small moon and the stars) clear of the text; the woven band as the horizon. Land: clay by day, terra roxa at night. The next activity typeset on the land, no card: "A próxima atividade" (Bricolage 750), its day circled by the urucum hand with "hoje"/"amanhã" in urucum, its title in Bricolage 800, who with the group's dot, when and where, the urucum "Mandar no WhatsApp". Then the pages as rows of type divided by thin rules: each name in its landscape colour's deep shade with its drawing and an arrow, and what it holds today (the lead story; the places with the layers' drawings; the businesses and their categories). Last, "O NOS acompanha o que 85 organizações de Dourados publicam" with the eight groups as dots.

STORY: The visitor reads the question, sees the next thing happening in the city and who organizes it, sends it to a WhatsApp group in one tap or opens it in the agenda, then scans what else NOS holds and opens a page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone 390×844: the bar (the mark, "Dourados", the theme button); the question in three lines (about 44px); the line under it; the lower bands with the sun at the right; the 40px band; "A próxima atividade", the circled day and "amanhã", the title (about 32px), who and when; "Mandar no WhatsApp" full width, above the bar at the foot. Desktop 1440×900: the bar with the links on the sky; the question in two lines (about 76px), the line under it, the big sun rising at the right; the band; the next activity at the left with its title at about 52px, "Mandar no WhatsApp" and "Ver na agenda" at the right; "Ver todas as N atividades"; the three pages' columns starting at the fold.

FORM: chosen by the user on 2026-11-12 after a round of five on the canvas (impeccable surface roll, seed 3c2ca719, mode persuade: A · Line-up, B · Quero…, C · Retratos dealt; D · Varal and E · Um dia the first two of the ranked list).
